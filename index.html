Notes

Poolish style

This calculator uses a Vito Iacopelli-inspired concept: a 100%-hydration poolish with a little honey, then final dough mixing, balling and cold fermentation. It is an adaptable calculator rather than a transcript of one specific video formula.

When the dough is ready

A mature dough ball is relaxed, slightly domed, aerated around the edges, and stretches without immediately snapping back. If it is still tight and dense, allow more room-temperature proofing. If it is extremely slack and fragile, handle gently and bake sooner.

Flour

For a hot outdoor pizza oven, 00 pizza flour is ideal if available. Strong all-purpose or bread flour also works. For a home oven, bread flour or strong AP flour is often easier to brown. Fine semolina is used mainly for peel dusting.
